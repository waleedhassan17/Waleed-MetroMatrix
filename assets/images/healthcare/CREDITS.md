# Health home photos

The photos on the patient Health home: the "How can we help you today?" cards and the service tiles. All are from Unsplash, free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): commercial use is allowed, and neither permission nor attribution is required. They are credited here as a courtesy and so they can be swapped out.

- **Size:** each is resized to 800 px on the long side and saved as JPEG (quality about 72, under 60 KB).
- **Rules:** every photo shows what its card does. No brand, logo or readable name appears in any of them.
- **Banners** are not bundled: staff choose them (`HealthcareBanner`, `/api/v1/admin/healthcare/banners`) and the app loads them from their URL. The seeded ones are credited in the backend's `scripts/seed-healthcare-home.js`.
